[center][size=4][b]English[/b][/size][/center]

If you are reading this pinned post, you probably have 85 mods, a log the size of a novel, and a strong feeling that Zzz_ReallyImportantStuff is to blame. It is not. Or it is. We did not check.

This is a separate Windows program, not a mod. You do not need to disable EAC. It does not download mods: it works with the archives already on your disk. It shows what is installed, what refuses to share settings with what, and what is actually happening in the log.

[B]System requirements and side effects[/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]RAM:[/B] no longer demands 16 GB. But it remembers.
[*][B]Clothing:[/B] after the first log analysis on 85 mods, changing your underwear is recommended. Dark colors are more practical.
[*][B]Nerves:[/B] a log with 85 mods is not debugging, it is a personality test.
[*][B]Compatibility:[/B] Windows 10/11 x64. Underwear compatibility is not guaranteed.
[*][B]Support:[/B] the author also screamed at the monitor. The monitor survived, development continues.
[/LIST]

[B]What it does[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Installs, updates, removes and backs up mods. The old folder stays until the replacement succeeds.
[*]Shows what is really in Mods and checks Nexus for updates if you provide your API key. The key is only for checking, not downloading.
[*]Reads logs: groups repeats, keeps the stack with the right error, and does not pick a culprit at random. If the log is silent, we are silent with it.
[/LIST]

[B]What it does not do[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Does not guarantee that all 85 mods will get along. Nobody can.
[*]Does not fix every conflict automatically. If it fixes one, it writes a separate patch mod.
[*]Does not promise no crashes. It promises you will know who to blame. Or at least suspect.
[/LIST]

If something goes wrong: Settings → Save diagnostics. Attach the file and mod names. Do not attach underwear photos, we cannot analyze those yet.

If the manager asks for 16 GB again, send diagnostics. An exorcist is optional.
